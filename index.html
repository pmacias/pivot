A conversational interview — not a quiz — that figures out which tech roles actually fit how your brain works. No email required. No multiple choice. Just a real conversation.

Runs locally in your browser. Your conversation is never stored.

Pivot is a two-phase career tool built specifically for scientists considering a move into tech.

Phase 1 conducts a real conversational interview — asking follow-up questions, probing inconsistencies, listening for what energizes you — then delivers a ranked role landscape with honest rationale and salary data.

Phase 2 (optional) is a skill gap assessment for any role you choose. It checks your existing skills against what that role requires, surfaces your gaps, and gives you a prioritized learning path.

Your data: nothing is stored. Everything runs in your browser. No account, no email, no tracking.
